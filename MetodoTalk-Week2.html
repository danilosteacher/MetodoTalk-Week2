Day 1 · W2 Connection Hub

He is / She is

Aprenda a falar sobre outras pessoas no seu ambiente de trabalho — cargo, localização e disponibilidade.

▶ Assistir Vídeo — Dia 1
🎯 Learning Outcomes — Day 1
  • Usar He is / She is para apresentar colegas de trabalho
  • Descrever cargo e localização usando to be na 3ª pessoa do singular
  • Entender que He / She muda com o gênero da pessoa, não com você
  • Produzir frases completas: nome + cargo + localização + status

📐 O Padrão — He is / She is
⚠️ Atenção — Erro mais comum
✅ Correto
  • He is an agronomist.
  • She is at the farm.
  • He is the manager.
❌ Erro comum
  • He are an agronomist.
  • She am at the farm.
  • He is a the manager.

🔑 He e She sempre usam IS — nunca am, nunca are.

📊 Estrutura — He is / She is

💡 Contrações: He is → He's  /  She is → She's

Day 2 · W2 Connection Hub

They are / This is / These are

Fale sobre grupos, apresente pessoas e substitua pronomes por cargos como um profissional.

▶ Assistir Vídeo — Dia 2
🎯 Learning Outcomes — Day 2
  • Usar They are para descrever grupos e equipes no agronegócio
  • Diferenciar This is (1 pessoa) de These are (2 ou mais)
  • Substituir He / She / They pelo cargo ou título da pessoa
  • Usar contrações: He's / She's / They're com naturalidade

📊 Estrutura — They are & contrações
EstruturaExemplo
They are + cargoThey are agronomists.
They are + origemThey are from Brasília.
They are + localizaçãoThey are at the branch.
They are + statusThey are ready for the project.

💡 Contração: He is → He's  /  She is → She's  /  They are → They're

⚠️ This is vs These are
THIS IS — 1 pessoa
  • This is Ana. She is an agronomist.
  • This is my manager, Dr. Souza.
THESE ARE — 2+ pessoas
  • These are Ana and Pedro.
  • These are the representatives.
🔄 Title Shift — Substitua o pronome pelo cargo

O verbo is / are não muda quando você substitui o pronome pelo cargo ou nome.

He is busy. → The manager is busy.
She is at the farm. → My coordinator is at the farm.
They are ready. → The clients are ready.
He is happy. → Paulo is happy.
📌 Diálogos — Apresentando a equipe
YOU:Hello! I am Ricardo. I am the sales manager. This is Ana. She is an agronomist. She is from the technical team. She is busy today, but she is ready.
YOU:Hello! I am the coordinator. This is my team. These are Paulo and Mary. They are agronomists. They are from Brasília. They are in the office today.

Day 3 · W2 Connection Hub

Perguntas & Integração

Conecte todas as formas do verbo to be — faça perguntas e responda sobre pessoas no seu trabalho.

▶ Assistir Vídeo — Dia 3
🎯 Learning Outcomes — Day 3
  • Fazer perguntas sobre outras pessoas: Is he...? / Is she...? / Are they...?
  • Responder de forma curta: Yes, he is. / No, she isn't.
  • Integrar I am / He is / She is / They are em conversas completas
  • Usar and / but para conectar informações sobre equipe

❓ Fazendo Perguntas com To Be
PerguntaSimNão
Is he ready?Yes, he is.No, he isn't.

Are they available?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.

⚡ isn't = is not  /  aren't = are not

🔗 Conectores — and / but
AND — adicionar info
  • She is an agronomist and she is ready.
  • He is at the farm and he is busy.
BUT — contraste
  • She is busy, but she is available.
  • He is at the branch, but he is online.

🌟 Produção Livre

Hoje você fala sobre situações reais. Use tudo que preparou essa semana — sua equipe, seus colegas, seu trabalho.

▶ Assistir Vídeo — Dia 5
🎯 Learning Outcomes — Day 5
  • Produzir apresentações completas usando toda a W2: He is / She is / They are / This is / These are
  • Descrever sua equipe real com cargo, localização e disponibilidade
  • Chegar pronto para a aula de conversação com conteúdo real da sua vida profissional

💡 Dica: Não existe resposta errada aqui. O objetivo é você produzir frases reais sobre seu contexto de trabalho. Use IA para ajudar se precisar — e leia em voz alta depois!
📸 Mini Project — The Team Photo
Imagine uma foto da sua equipe. Descreva-a usando os padrões da semana.

Use os chunks do vocabulário de hoje: This is... / These are... / He/She is... / They are...

Step 1 — Você
Apresente-se: nome, cargo, equipe/departamento, cidade.
I am Fernanda. I am the operations coordinator. I am from Ribeirão Preto. I am from the technical team.
Step 2 — Um colega do sexo masculino
Apresente um colega homem: nome, cargo, localização e status hoje.
This is Carlos. He is a veterinarian. He is from Cuiabá. He is from the operations team. He is in the field today, but he is ready for the project.
Step 3 — Uma colega do sexo feminino
Apresente uma colega mulher: nome, cargo, status atual.
This is Laura. She is an agronomist. She is from Portugal. She is from the commercial team. She is busy today, but she is available for the meeting.
Step 4 — Sua equipe (They are)
Apresente 2 ou 3 colegas juntos como equipe. Use "These are... They are..."
These are Carlos and Laura. They are my colleagues. They are agronomists. They are from the technical team. They are ready for the farm visit.
🗣️ Perguntas para a Aula de Conversação
Prepare suas respostas — o professor vai perguntar isso na aula!
1. Who is on your team right now?
Fale sobre 2–3 pessoas da sua equipe real. Use He is / She is / They are.
On my team, there is Ana. She is the sales coordinator. She is from Campinas. And there is Roberto. He is a field representative. He is busy today — he is at the farm.
2. Is your manager available right now? Where is he/she?
Fale sobre a disponibilidade do seu gerente ou supervisor.
My manager is Dr. Figueiredo. He is the operations director. He is not available right now — he is at a meeting in São Paulo. But he is online.
3. Introduce your best colleague to a foreign partner.
Imagine que você está apresentando um colega a um parceiro internacional. Faça a apresentação completa.
This is Mariana. She is our technical analyst. She is from Goiânia and she is from the crop protection team. She is our specialist — she is the right person for your project!
